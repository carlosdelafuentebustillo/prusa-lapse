--- Prusa-lapse Custom G-Code Snippet (Firmware v1.2.2+) ---

NOTE: Starting in Firmware v1.2.2, Prusa-lapse features Hybrid Triggering:
- Mode A (Zero-Config Smart Z-Height): If you slice normally WITHOUT any custom G-code, the cameras automatically detect confirmed layer Z-height advances via PrusaLink telemetry (filtering out travel Z-hops and MBL probing) and capture each layer on the fly!
- Mode B (Parked Extruder G-Code): Paste the snippet below into PrusaSlicer if you want the extruder parked out of the way in the back-right corner for every frame. Once the camera detects M220 S99 (or M221 S99), it automatically locks into Parked G-Code mode for the remainder of the print.

INSTRUCTIONS (for Mode B - Parked Extruder):
1. Open PrusaSlicer.
2. Go to the "Printer Settings" tab.
3. Make sure your current printer profile (the one linked to Prusa Connect) is selected in the dropdown.
4. Click on "Custom G-code" in the left menu.
5. Scroll down to the "After layer change G-code" text box.
6. Copy the code below and paste it at the bottom of that text box.
7. Click the "Save" icon next to your printer profile name (e.g. name it "Original Prusa MK4S - Timelapse"). This saves a variation of your profile that is STILL linked to your physical Prusa Connect printer!

--- COPY EVERYTHING BELOW THIS LINE ---

; --- Prusa-lapse Layer Change Trigger (Parked Mode) ---
{if layer_num > 1}
M83 ; Relative extruder mode
G1 E-0.8 F2100 ; Retract 0.8mm (Direct Drive / Nextruder safe)
G91 ; Relative positioning
G1 Z0.4 F1200 ; Lift Z by 0.4mm to clear printed part
G90 ; Absolute positioning
G1 X240 Y200 F9000 ; Park printhead at back-right corner
M400 ; Wait for park move to complete
G4 P300 ; Wait 300ms for frame vibrations to settle
M220 S99 ; Signal Prusa-lapse cameras (M221 S99 also supported)
G4 P1200 ; Wait 1.2s for high-res UXGA capture
M220 S100 ; Restore 100% speed factor
G1 Z{layer_z} F1200 ; Restore exact layer Z height
G1 E0.8 F2100 ; Un-retract filament
{endif}
; --- End Prusa-lapse Trigger ---
