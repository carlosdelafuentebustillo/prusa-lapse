--- Prusa-lapse Custom G-Code Snippet ---

Since importing a .ini file creates a brand new printer profile (which disconnects from your Prusa Connect physical printer), the best way to use Prusa-lapse is to simply paste this G-Code into your EXISTING Prusa Connect printer profile.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Open PrusaSlicer.
2. Go to the "Printer Settings" tab.
3. Make sure your current printer profile (the one linked to Prusa Connect) is selected in the dropdown.
4. Click on "Custom G-code" in the left menu.
5. Scroll down to the "After layer change G-code" text box.
6. Copy the code below and paste it at the bottom of that text box.
7. Click the "Save" icon next to your printer profile name (e.g. name it "Original Prusa MK4S - Timelapse"). This saves a variation of your profile that is STILL linked to your physical Prusa Connect printer!

--- COPY EVERYTHING BELOW THIS LINE ---

; --- Prusa-lapse Layer Change Trigger ---
{if layer_num > 0}
G1 E-2.0 F2100 ; Retract 2mm to prevent stringing
G1 X240 Y210 F6000 ; Move to park position (back-right corner)
G4 P500 ; Wait 500ms for vibrations to settle
M220 S99 ; Signal for the Prusa-lapse monitor
G4 P2000 ; Wait 2 seconds for cameras to capture
M220 S100 ; Clear LCD message / restore speed factor
G1 E2.0 F2100 ; Un-retract
{endif}
; --- End Prusa-lapse Trigger ---
